About

Geography, environmental planning and GIS in one profile.

I am a Geospatial Analyst supporting conservation projects in Germany and several African countries with data management, geospatial analyses and cartographic products such as dashboards and web and print maps.

Before that, I led environmental planning projects within an international engineering and consulting company of Deutsche Bahn and administered the company's ArcGIS Enterprise system. That mix lets me connect legal requirements, complex project areas and spatial analysis into decisions people can rely on.
